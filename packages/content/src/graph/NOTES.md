# v1 graph content notes

JSON cannot carry comments, so the rationale for `v1.json` lives here.

- **Counts.** 12 EAR, 12 READ, 7 HANDS, 3 BYEAR, 1 APPLY. READ and HANDS each include one R10 node (`read.minor.accidentals`, `hands.minor.leadingTone`). Three of the READ nodes are the treble nodes added in TP.2.
- **Bass landmarks.** Spec: "low C, F (clef line), middle C, low G". Chosen pitches: C2 (low C), F3 (the F line of the bass clef), C4 (middle C), G2 (low G). Scientific pitch notation, middle C = C4.
- **`read.bass.skips` full range.** `fullRange: ["C2", "E4"]` covers the bass staff plus two ledger lines above.
- **Key dependence.** EAR and BYEAR false. READ and HANDS true, except `read.rhythm.basic` (rhythm only, single pitch) which is false.
- **Mastery defaults.** `minAccuracy 0.9`, `windowAttempts 20`, `distinctSessions 2`, `maxMedianLatencyMs` equal to the node `targetLatencyMs`.
- **Rhythm nodes.** `ear.rhythm.echo` and `read.rhythm.basic` have no latency criterion: they are scored on onset deviation, not response speed. Reading uses a window of 15.
- **HANDS and BYEAR.** Whole tasks are slow (spec 5.4), so `windowAttempts 5`, `minAccuracy 0.8` (4 of 5; 0.9 over 5 would demand a perfect run), no latency.
- **Alignment-scored reading and `ear.real.phrase`.** Window of 10, no latency. `read.grand.2line` 0.85, `read.grand.sight` and `ear.real.phrase` 0.8, because whole-line scoring is harsher than single-note scoring.
- **Target latencies (ms).** Single degrees 1500 (spec), bass landmarks/steps 1200 (spec, bass note naming), bass skips/intervals/minor accidentals 1500, bass chords 2500, triad quality 2000, cells 3000, bass motion 4000 (several roots), progressions and cadences 3000 (spec).
- **Generators.** Degree nodes list `degreeSing` first, then `degreeId`. Nothing is sung any more: both play the degree and `cellEcho` is played too, so none of them requires the microphone (`contentVersion` 2026.10.6). Implemented: the four ear generators (T2.6) and `bassNoteRead` and `rhythmRead` (T3.15, `contentVersion` 2026.10.2). Everything else is `implemented: false` until its milestone (`bassChordRead`, `sightLine` in M5).
- **Away eligibility (spec 6.5).** True for EAR generators, `bassNoteRead` (locate on an on-screen keyboard) and `concept`. False for `rhythmRead`, `bassChordRead`, `sightLine`, `lhPattern`, `lhPatternVariants`, `lhPatternMinor`, `coordTask`, `byEarPhrase`.
- **Seed durations (seconds per item).** degreeSing 8, degreeId 6, cellEcho 12, rhythmEcho 12, rhythmRead 12, bassMotion 15, chordQuality 8, progressionId 15, bassNoteRead 5, bassChordRead 8, sightLine 60, lhPattern 30 (T5.3), lhPatternVariants 30 (T5.7), lhPatternMinor 30, coordTask 90, byEarPhrase 90 (T7.2), byEarHarmonise 240, byEarRecording 240, concept 30.
- **`ear.cadence`.** Reuses `progressionId` with a `cadences` param (two-chord endings) rather than a separate generator.
- **`contentVersion`** must change whenever graph semantics change.
- **Reading enabled (T3.15).** `bassNoteRead` enables `read.bass.landmarks`, `read.bass.steps`, `read.bass.skips`, `read.bass.intervals` and `read.minor.accidentals` (R18); `rhythmRead` enables `read.rhythm.basic`. `read.bass.chords`, `read.grand.2line` and `read.grand.sight` stay disabled. Flipping the flags changes graph semantics, so `contentVersion` moved from 2026.10.1 to 2026.10.2 and stored snapshots are discarded and refolded (R8).
- **Bass motion enabled (TP.1).** `bassMotion` enables `ear.bass.I-IV-V-vi`. An item is a four-chord progression from I, IV, V and vi that starts on I and never repeats a chord twice running (27 items); the learner plays the four bass roots as a pitch-class sequence. `contentVersion` moved to 2026.10.3.
- **Triad quality enabled (TP.1).** `chordQuality` enables `ear.quality.triad`. An item is a triad of one quality on one of the degrees 1 to 6 of the major scale, root position with the root doubled in the bass, held for two beats after the cadence (`qualities` x `roots` = 12 items). The learner picks the quality with buttons (R21). `laterQualities` (diminished) is not in the universe yet: add it to `qualities` in a later graph version. `contentVersion` moved to 2026.10.4.
- **Treble reading enabled (TP.2).** `trebleNoteRead` enables `read.treble.landmarks` (C4, G4, C5: middle C, the G the clef curls round, the C above), `read.treble.steps` and `read.treble.skips` (the whole range C4 to A5, one ledger line below the staff to one above). They mirror the bass nodes: key dependent, the same mastery and latency targets, the same generator rules (R15, R17). The generator is the bass one with a different staff (`makeNoteRead`). The nodes sit after the other READ nodes in the file, so the composer still introduces bass landmarks and rhythm before treble landmarks (it breaks ties by file order); move them earlier to change that. Treble has no interval or minor accidental nodes yet and is not part of placement. `contentVersion` moved to 2026.10.5.
- **Pieces enabled (TP.5, `contentVersion` 2026.10.8).** `apply.pieces` is the one node in the APPLY strand. Every piece phrase is an item of it (R22); its `mastery` block is a placeholder because the node's own state is never used. `pieceLine` is implemented, but it is built over the piece library in `core`, so the app injects it into the composer and the session runtime (see `apps/web/src/lib/pieces.ts`) rather than the core registry holding it. Seed duration 25 s, not Away eligible. A piece is offered once every reading node it needs is `fluent` (see `docs/licences.md` for the tunes).
- **Pieces by ear enabled (TP.6, `contentVersion` 2026.10.9).** `apply.piece-ear` is the second APPLY node, with `pieceEar` (built over the piece library, injected like `pieceLine`). Its items are the same phrases as `apply.pieces`, but they are only offered for a piece once every phrase of it has been played correctly. Sung part then played part, both must be right (R12); the score is revealed after the answer. Seed duration 60 s, not Away eligible. Like `apply.pieces`, its `mastery` block is a placeholder.
- **Left-hand chords enabled (T5.3, `contentVersion` 2026.10.12).** `lhPattern` enables `hands.lh.triads.root` only (R26). An item is a progression of four root-position triads (`fam=A|prog=I-IV-V-I`, the `bassMotion` universe times three key families, 81 items); the key and register are presentation. It is played as block chords on a MIDI keyboard and graded by alignment (`chordLine`). `lhPattern` now requires `keys`, `polyphonic` and `notation` (the chords are drawn on the bass staff) and its seed duration is 30 s, down from 90 s (a progression is about 8 s of playing plus a 4 s count-in). The four other nodes that listed `lhPattern` (`hands.lh.inversions`, `hands.lh.broken`, `hands.lh.pop`, `hands.minor.leadingTone`) now list the new unimplemented `lhPatternVariants` (seed 90 s, `keys` and `polyphonic`), so enabling the root-position node does not enable them. Concept card `concept.hands.lh.triads.root`. The node is `available` for every learner from the start (no prerequisites), but the composer only offers or introduces it when the session's input is MIDI.
- **Left-hand patterns enabled (T5.7, `contentVersion` 2026.10.13).** `lhPatternVariants` enables `hands.lh.inversions`, `hands.lh.broken` and `hands.lh.pop` (R27, MIDI only, the same `requires` as `lhPattern`: `keys`, `polyphonic`, `notation`; seed 30 s, not Away eligible). Each node gained `chords: ["I", "IV", "V", "vi"]`, so its progressions are the `lhPattern` ones (the `bassMotion` universe, per key family). Inversions items are `fam=A|prog=I-IV-V-I` (81 items): block chords with close voice leading, the first in root position and each next one the root position, first or second inversion that moves the hand least (ties to the bass nearest A2, so the hand does not drift), graded as a `chordLine` with slash symbols such as `F/C`. Broken and pop items carry the pattern, `fam=A|pattern=alberti|prog=I-IV-V-I` (162 items each): broken 1-3-5-3 and Alberti 1-5-3-5 in eighths, root-5-8 (quarter, eighth, eighth) and a syncopated root-5-8 (eighth, quarter, eighth), two beats per chord, graded as a `line`. Everything stays in C2 to C4; patterns that reach the octave keep their roots at C3 or below. `hands.minor.leadingTone` moved to the new unimplemented `lhPatternMinor`, so it stays disabled. Concept cards `concept.hands.lh.inversions`, `concept.hands.lh.broken` and `concept.hands.lh.pop`, and milestones for the three nodes. `hands.coord.moving` still waits for `coordTask`.
- **Progression and cadence ear enabled (T7.1, `contentVersion` 2026.10.10).** `progressionId` enables `ear.prog.core`, `ear.cadence`, `ear.prog.minor` and `ear.prog.colour`. An item is one option of the node (R1): a progression of the node's `progressions` (`prog=I-V-vi-IV`) or a named cadence of its `cadences` map (`cadence=plagal`), so the universes are 4, 4, 3 and 3. The key is a presentation parameter and never part of the id (R2: the presentation carries the tonic, the voiced chord notes and the seed). A progression is heard after the key-setting cadence (I-IV-V-I, or i-iv-V-i for the minor node); a cadence pair is heard after a short I-ii lead-in instead, voiced together with it, so the pair sounds like the end of a phrase. Chords come from `theory.progression`, which now knows the minor numerals (i iv v VI III VII V, with V major on the raised 7th) and the colour chords (V/V, borrowed iv, bVII). The learner picks the answer with buttons in both modes (R21), labelled with the numerals ("I-V-vi-IV") or the cadence name ("Perfect", "Plagal", "Imperfect", "Deceptive"). A wrong answer reads "You chose plagal; it was perfect", and the question reads "Which one did you hear?" for numeral labels. Concept cards `concept.prog.core`, `concept.cadence`, `concept.prog.minor` and `concept.prog.colour`; the card `progression` example gained an optional `mode` for the minor card. The nodes stay locked until `ear.quality.triad` progresses (the prerequisite chain is unchanged).
- **By-ear melodies enabled (T7.2, `contentVersion` 2026.10.11).** `byEarPhrase` enables `byear.melody.simple` only (R25). An item is a melody slot (`bars=4|slot=1`, 3 lengths x 6 slots = 18 items), a pure function of its id; the key and register are presentation (R1, R2). Stages: hear after the cadence, sing, play, then the score is revealed; both parts must be right (R12). `byEarPhrase` now requires `mic`, `keys` and `notation` (the reveal draws a score) and no longer `polyphonic`; seed duration 90 s (a 4 bar melody sung and played takes a minute or two). `byear.harmonise` moved to the new unimplemented `byEarHarmonise` (roots and chords stages, polyphonic input) and `ear.real.phrase` and `byear.full.phrase` to the new unimplemented `byEarRecording`, so enabling the melody stage does not enable them or ship synthetic tunes as "real recordings". Concept card `concept.byear.melody.simple`. The `melody` stage is the only one the node lists; bass and chord stages, "find the tonic" and cross-session stage gating are not built. The node stays locked until `ear.cells.major.2-4` is mastered.
